CRITICAL DESIGN DECISIONS & TRADEOFFS

COMPANY NAMES PRESERVED

Entities like "KSH International Limited", "ICICI Securities", and "HDFC Bank" appear hundreds of times. Redacting legal party names makes IPO filings unreadable. Configurable via config.py.

TOLL-FREE NUMBERS PRESERVED

Numbers like 1800 267 3225 are public customer-care lines (SEBI SCORES, registrar helpdesks), not individual personal identifiers. Direct lines and mobiles are redacted.

DIN REDACTED VS CIN PRESERVED

DIN (Director Identification Number) identifies an individual human director and is treated as sensitive PII. CIN (Corporate Identity Number) identifies the legal company and is preserved.
