⚙️ Architecture & Pipeline

Four-stage deterministic pipeline handling complex multi-table Word documents.

01
Document Inventory
Parses 3,991 text blocks across paragraphs and 76 tables, recording exact run-level character boundaries.
02
Hybrid Detectors
Runs pre-compiled regex, table column inspectors, and spaCy NER with 60+ financial denylist filters.
03
Overlap Resolution
Greedy longest-match span resolver prevents nested or conflicting redaction collisions.
04
Run-Level Injection
Applies seeded Faker replacements directly to docx XML runs, preserving font styles, bolding, and colors.

⚖️ Key Design Decisions & Tradeoffs

Carefully calibrated choices tailored specifically for Indian IPO filings.

🏢 Company Names Preserved by Default

Entity names like "KSH International Limited", "ICICI Securities", and "HDFC Bank" appear hundreds of times. Full redaction makes legal filings unreadable. Detection is implemented and configurable via config.py.

📞 Toll-Free Lines Preserved

Public support lines (e.g. 1800 267 3225) are general public customer-care hotlines, not private individual identifiers. Direct mobile and landline numbers with +91 are redacted.

🆔 DIN Redacted vs CIN Preserved

DIN (Director Identification Number) identifies an individual human director and is treated as PII. CIN (Corporate Identity Number) identifies the legal company entity and is preserved.
